The goal of this augmented reality project is to serve as an educational guide, helping users learn more about the history of The Abbey of Our Lady of New Clairvaux. Our team worked alongside Brother Luis Cortez, director of development and volunteer programs, as he highlighted his experience by walking us through behind-the-scenes details and the history, and sharing his personal experiences at the abbey. Our product, Abbey of New Clairvaux AR tour, provides an accessible, self-guided, educational tour of the property, with interactive elements users can tap to view historical information about each stop. The self-guided tour is accessed via your smartphone: scan the QR code on a brochure provided by the abbey to open the website application. From there, users can tap through different stops of their choice within the property. When they arrive at a stop via the website application, they can scan items using the camera on their device to get a more in-depth explanation of the history of this specific stop. Overall, this AR tool allows users to explore their personal interests within the property and engage in an interactive experience.
