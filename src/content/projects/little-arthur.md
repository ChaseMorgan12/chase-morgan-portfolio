Little Arthur is a 1-4 player local co-op ARPG that focuses on blending fast based combat with physics based puzzles. Work together with friends or play alone to navigate 3 unique levels and reclaim the Holy Grail. Each level is inhabited by a variety of enemies, led by a powerful boss unique to each level. Explore the branching pathways to find rewards and other challenges.
